# Action Center

Short-lived work moving between you and floThink, kept separate from long-term
knowledge folders.

- **My Inbox** — files, results, and updates for you to review.
- **Agent Inbox** — files and requests waiting for floThink to process.
- **Outbox** — finished items ready to download, share, or send.

Process both inboxes regularly. Keep canonical knowledge in its matching
`KNOWLEDGE`, `PLAN`, `AUTOMATE`, or `SYSTEM` folder, not in the Action Center.
floThink may hide an empty Outbox.
