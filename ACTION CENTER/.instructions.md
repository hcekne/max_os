# Action Center Instructions

Use the three folders according to who needs to act next.

- Put user-facing results and updates in `My Inbox/`.
- Read work requests and source files from `Agent Inbox/`.
- Put externally ready deliverables in `Outbox/` only when they are ready to
  download, share, or send.
- Move durable knowledge into its canonical workspace folder.
- Move reviewed or processed transient files into the matching path below
  `SYSTEM/Cleaning/Archive/` or `SYSTEM/Cleaning/Rubbish Bin/`.
