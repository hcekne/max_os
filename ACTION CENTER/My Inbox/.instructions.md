# My Inbox Instructions

This folder is the owner's review queue.

- Put agent results, workflow deliveries, and updates requiring the owner's
  attention here.
- Do not treat an item as approved merely because it appears here.
- Preserve links to the canonical source files that produced a result.

Review algorithm:

1. List `ACTION CENTER/My Inbox/` directly.
2. Summarize what each item is, where it came from, and what decision or
   action the owner needs to take.
3. After the owner reviews an item, route durable material to its canonical
   folder and move the transient copy to Archive or Rubbish Bin according to
   retention value.
