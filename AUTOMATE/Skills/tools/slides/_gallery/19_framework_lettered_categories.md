1. **Member-risk tracking.** Continuously monitoring clinical and behavioural risk signals across the member base.
2. **Population analytics.** Aggregating outcomes data into provider scorecards and intervention prioritisation.
3. **Decision support.** Surfacing claims-handling and triage recommendations to case managers at the point of decision.
4. **Claims auto-adjudication.** Closing the simple, low-risk claims without human review.
5. **Care-pathway routing.** Steering members into the right intervention tier based on the risk model.
6. **Network optimisation.** Tuning provider mix and capacity in near-real time against demand.
