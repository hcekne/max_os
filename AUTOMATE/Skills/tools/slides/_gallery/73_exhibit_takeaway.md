- **Margins are compressing fastest where decisions are still manual.** Pricing and assortment calls made weekly, not daily, leave roughly two points of margin on the table each season.

- **The capability gap compounds.** Competitors that automated demand sensing 18 months ago now react to shifts in days; the lag widens every cycle the gap is left unaddressed.

- **The investment window is open but narrowing.** Cloud and tooling costs have fallen far enough that a focused build pays back inside a year, before talent and vendor pricing tighten again.
