# Workflow - Draft LinkedIn Newsletter from Thesis

## Goal
- Convert one strong thesis into a publishable LinkedIn newsletter draft with clear argument flow and practical credibility.

## Inputs
- Thesis statement (1 sentence)
- Target audience
- 3-5 core claims
- Real examples from your work
- Any external timeline/fact anchors

## Steps
1. **Define the narrative tension**
   - Old consensus vs current reality.
   - One sentence: “Most people think X, but Y is now true.”

2. **Set the article spine**
   - Hook
   - In Brief (3-4 bullets)
   - Main argument sections
   - Counterpoints
   - Conclusion

3. **Add evidence layers**
   - Timeline facts
   - Operator examples
   - Economic frame (ROI logic)

4. **Write in scannable blocks**
   - Short sections
   - Strong headers
   - Concrete language over abstract claims

5. **Stress-test credibility**
   - Remove overclaims
   - Add caveats/counterpoints
   - Keep confident but non-hypey tone

6. **Create publication variants**
   - Full newsletter version
   - Short LinkedIn post teaser
   - Optional closing line variants

## Outputs
- v01 newsletter draft (publishable)
- 3-4 bullet “In Brief” summary
- Optional teaser post for cross-promotion

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Clear tension in first 2 paragraphs
- [ ] “In Brief” section included
- [ ] Main argument flows logically
- [ ] Timeline and examples integrated
- [ ] Counterpoints included
- [ ] Conclusion has one strong take-away line
