# Knowledge

Long-lived knowledge about your work belongs here.

- `People/` — people you work with and context worth remembering.
- `Organizations/` — companies and organizations connected to your work.
- `Clients/` — client relationships, account context, and commitments.
- `Projects/` — active and completed work organized by project.
- `Content/` — articles, presentations, videos, and publishing work.
- `Interactions/` — meetings, calls, decisions, and follow-ups.
- `Notes/` — ideas and research that do not yet belong elsewhere.

Custom long-lived folders also belong directly inside `KNOWLEDGE/`.
