# Outbox Instructions

Materials staged for delivery to another actor, agent, or external recipient.

- Only place items here when they are ready to be picked up.
- Prefer one item per file; group multi-file deliveries under a single subfolder.
- Use `<recipient>/` subfolders when delivering to multiple downstream destinations.
- Cross-link each outbox item back to the canonical source note(s) that produced it.
- After delivery, move items to `16_Cleaning/Archive/17_Outbox/` (historical value) or `16_Cleaning/Rubbish Bin/17_Outbox/` (low retention).
- When scanning the outbox, list directories directly before relying on globbed search.
- Do not use the outbox as a working area; canonical drafts belong with the project, not in the outbox.
- Do not duplicate canonical content into the outbox. Prefer rendered or packaged deliverables that reference the canonical source.
