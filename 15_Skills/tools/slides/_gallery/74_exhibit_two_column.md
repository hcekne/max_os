<h3>Where the value is</h3>

- **Higher drop density** cuts cost per parcel by routing on live demand, not fixed plans.
- **Fewer failed deliveries** through predicted time windows and proactive customer nudges.
- **Lower fleet idle time** by rebalancing vehicles across depots through the day.
- **Faster peak response** when volumes spike around promotions and holidays.

</div>

<div class="col">
<h3>What it requires</h3>

- **Clean telemetry** from every vehicle and handheld, unified in one stream.
- **A live demand signal** that updates intra-day, not an overnight batch.
- **Dispatcher buy-in**, with the model recommending and humans approving.
- **A product team** that owns routing outcomes end to end, not a one-off build.
