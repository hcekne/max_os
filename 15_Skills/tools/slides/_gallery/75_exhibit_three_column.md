<h3>As a monetisation lever</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Dynamic paywall.</strong> Per-reader propensity scoring lifts subscriber conversion on long-form titles.</li>
<li><strong>Contextual ad targeting.</strong> First-party signals replace third-party cookies ahead of deprecation.</li>
<li><strong>Premium tiers.</strong> AI-curated briefings packaged as a paid B2B feed.</li>
</ul>

<h3>As a new capability</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Newsroom copilots.</strong> Draft, fact-check and localise at desk speed across 9 markets.</li>
<li><strong>Archive search.</strong> 40 years of content made queryable for editors and licensing.</li>
<li><strong>Audience insight.</strong> Topic-trend detection feeds the commissioning calendar.</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="col3">
<h3>As an enabler</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Governance layer.</strong> Provenance and rights tracking on every generated asset.</li>
<li><strong>Shared platform.</strong> One model-access gateway, audited and rate-limited.</li>
<li><strong>Skills uplift.</strong> Editorial and commercial teams trained on safe, on-brand use.</li>
</ul>
