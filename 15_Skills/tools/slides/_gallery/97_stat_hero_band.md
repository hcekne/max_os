Halcyon Retail processes 4.2m returned items a year, and roughly a third are resold below cost or written off entirely. Closing half of that gap (through better sizing guidance, faster grading, and resale routing) recovers the figure on the left.

That is more than the projected three-year EBIT contribution of the entire new-store programme, for a fraction of the capital.
