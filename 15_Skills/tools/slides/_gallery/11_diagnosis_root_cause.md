1. **ID verification UX.** Customers are asked for the same proof of address twice; abandonment doubles between step 3 and step 4.
2. **Soft-search ambiguity.** The "this won't affect your credit score" copy reads as evasive in user testing; trust collapses on mobile.
3. **Handover friction.** Joint-account applicants are pushed to a branch flow that takes 11 days on average to schedule.
4. **No save-and-resume.** Applicants who break off can't return to the same point; only 18% restart the funnel within 7 days.
