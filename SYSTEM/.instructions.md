This folder is the Max OS system kernel.

- `Actor.md` — who owns this workspace (identity; stable).
- `State.md` — what is currently true (working memory; changes often).
- `Memory.md` — durable decisions and preferences (curated; newer replaces
  older).
- `Log.md` — what happened (episodic; append-only, one dated bullet after
  meaningful work; never rewrite past entries).
- `Policy.md` — how workspace information is handled (rare, deliberate
  change).
- `Templates/` — structures to copy for new documents.
- `Proposals/` — changes awaiting owner approval.
- `Cleaning/` — Archive and Rubbish Bin surfaces (rules in `Policy.md`).
- `Storage References/` — app-generated links to large stored files.

The kernel is closed: do not create new top-level documents in `SYSTEM/`.
New abilities are skills, workflows, or modules in `AUTOMATE/`; new document
shapes are templates; new policy is a section of `Policy.md`; suggestions
for system change go to `Proposals/`.
