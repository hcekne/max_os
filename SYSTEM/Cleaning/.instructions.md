Central retention surface. Rules live in `SYSTEM/Policy.md` (Archive and
Rubbish Bin section).

- `Archive/` — historically useful material, kept long-term. Mirror the
  original source path beneath the root; record significant moves in
  `Archive/Index.md`.
- `Rubbish Bin/` — clearly stale, superseded, or low-value material queued
  for deletion. In the MaxOS app, binned files land flat at the root and
  Empty deletes them immediately — never bin anything high-retention.
- Final deliverables, contracts, invoices, legal/commercial documents, and
  client-provided materials default to `Archive/`, never `Rubbish Bin/`.
